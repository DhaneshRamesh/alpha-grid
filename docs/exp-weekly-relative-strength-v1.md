**Mechanism and null.** Persistent capital allocation to recent winners may continue for several days because institutions trade gradually, while a small account can enter without the impact of a large fund. Competing explanations are common market beta, survivorship, earnings and reversal. Hypothesis: buying the highest positive trailing 60-session return divided by its trailing realized volatility yields positive conservative net delivery reference return over the next four regular sessions and beats a same-day median-rank stock. Null: either conservative selected mean net or paired selected-minus-median mean net is <=0. Jegadeesh and Titman (1993) find longer-horizon US momentum; this four-session NSE consequence is an unverified hypothesis, not a replication: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1993.tb04702.x. The current NSE Indices momentum whitepaper is context, not proof for the 2023–2025 sample: https://www.niftyindices.com/docs/default-source/indices/nifty500-momentum-50/momentum-strategy-whitepaper_2026.pdf
