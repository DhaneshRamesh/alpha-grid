# EXP-OPENING-SELL-PRESSURE-REVERSAL-V1

Frozen 2026-11-08 13:55 IST, before evaluating this rule's outcomes. Exploratory development test; DATA_ONLY / NO TRADE. Do not inspect post-2025-10-31 data or TCS, KOTAKBANK, MARUTI, BHEL, DIVISLAB.

## Mechanism choice

1. **Opening sell-pressure reversal (chosen).** A liquidity-motivated opening sale may briefly outrun patient bids; inventory and adverse-selection risk can delay correction. The alternative is informed selling that continues. Existing one-minute OHLCV can test later price references, though it cannot identify the seller, spread, first candle availability or fills. A 50-bp opening decline leaves room for a 20–30-bp partial reversal to clear modeled costs. Reject if conditional gross and conservative net do not beat a same-day control. Heston, Korajczyk and Sadka (2010) document short-lived liquidity reversals in US stocks, not an NSE guarantee: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2010.01573.x
2. **Informed opening-order continuation.** Institutions may split informed orders; slow assimilation could sustain direction. OHLCV can test price continuation, but prior Alpha Grid 15- and 60-minute long-momentum/breakout reference economics were negative. It remains observable but less promising at the same cost burden. The existence of informed flow cannot be established from candles.
3. **Passive spread capture after order-book depletion.** Urgent market orders pay for immediacy while liquidity suppliers earn spread; queue risk and adverse selection could sustain a premium. The historical OHLCV archive cannot establish displayed spread, queue place or maker fills, so this is not testable here. Cont, Kukanov and Stoikov (2014) use genuine order-book events, not bar volume, for this mechanism: https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6402

Ranking for this offline run by observability, economic plausibility, execution feasibility and falsifiability: reversal first, continuation second, passive spread capture third. This is a research-priority ranking, not a performance ranking.

## Frozen test

- **Hypothesis:** After a >=50-bp fall from the 09:15 opening price to the 09:59 close, buying the single most-declined eligible stock has positive 120-minute conditional net reference return and beats a same-day, same-time less-declined control after conservative costs. **Null:** either mean conditional net <=0 or paired net improvement <=0.
- **Universe:** Frozen November 2 liquidity-rank 1–5 NSE_EQ stocks only: HDFCBANK 1333, RELIANCE 2885, INFY 1594, ICICIBANK 4963, BHARTIARTL 10604. The dated 2026 universe is a retrospective selection; historical membership and liquidity continuity are not proven. No substitute names.
- **Partitions:** 2023-11-01 through 2025-04-30 is an earlier fixed-rule consistency check; 2025-05-01 through 2025-10-31 is the primary *inspected development* result. Neither is a pristine holdout. Never read source response bytes for later dates or the five excluded names.
- **Session/data filters:** Weekday ordinary 09:15–15:30 IST sessions only; exclude dates in the repository's `NSE_SPECIAL_SESSIONS`; require complete one-minute 09:15–09:59 feature bars and continuous 10:00–12:11 reference path for both selected and control stocks. Check original file byte count/SHA-256, schema, monotonic unique timestamps, OHLCV validity, identity and request bounds with existing verifier before using it. Reject invalid windows; never silently deduplicate or repair. Exclude known corporate-action ex-dates and the preceding observed regular session for each instrument. No interday return feature. The August 2026 CAS change is outside both partitions; a prospective replication would need fresh CAS evidence.
- **Decision/selection:** At 10:05 IST, under the *assumed, unverified* start-of-minute label and <=300-second post-completion availability, compute only `(C_09:59/O_09:15-1)*10,000`. Eligible shock is <=-50 bps. Pick the most negative shock, tie by symbol. One selected stock per day. Select the same-day control among the other four with the smallest absolute opening return, tie by symbol. Selection uses no future bars, volume, fitted parameter or model score. If no shock or no control, NO_TRADE.
- **Reference outcome:** Fixed ₹5,000 maximum buy notional, integer shares. Buy reference is the 10:06 bar open; sell reference is the 12:06 bar open, 120 minutes later. Do not use highs/lows as fills. If either path is incomplete, censor the entire pair and report the count without replacement. Fixed timing stress: 10:11 open to 12:11 open, with no reselection.
- **Costs:** Compute Dhan intraday buy/sell brokerage, STT, exchange transaction, SEBI, IPFT, GST and stamp duty with `quant.research.prospective.compute_dhan_intraday_fees` on actual integer-share notionals. Separately assume round-trip spread plus residual execution friction of 5+5 bps (base) and 10+10 bps (conservative), both on buy notional. These are sensitivity assumptions, not observed NSE quotes or fills.
- **Primary metric:** Mean selected net INR and bps per opportunity in the later partition under conservative costs, plus mean paired selected-minus-control net INR. NO_TRADE is zero. Report gross, each cost, opportunity and censor counts, favorable >=+30-bp and adverse <=-30-bp reference rates, 5th/50th/95th percentiles, worst loss, stock/date concentration, and cumulative reference drawdown. One opportunity per session prevents intraday overlap.
- **Uncertainty:** 2,000 paired session-date bootstrap resamples with seed 20261108; preserve selected/control pairs. Report two-sided 95% intervals for selected mean net and paired net difference. Date clustering does not remove multi-day dependence or retrospective universe bias.
- **Acceptance for *further prospective validation only*:** At least 30 selected independent dates in the later period, at least three symbols represented and no symbol >60% of selected dates; both conservative-cost 95% lower bounds >0; earlier-period mean selected and paired net >0; 10:11 timing-stress conservative mean selected net >0. Even a pass is exploratory, never an order authorization.
- **Reject and stop:** If later-period conservative mean selected net <=0 or paired improvement <=0, or timing stress erases it. If fewer than 30 selected dates, classify INCONCLUSIVE and stop. No threshold/horizon/stock search after observing results. Any invalid raw source or identity breach stops before outcome analysis.

Historical candle availability, achievable spread/slippage, borrow and fills remain unverified. The test can reject this exact reference-price mechanism but cannot validate executable profitability.
