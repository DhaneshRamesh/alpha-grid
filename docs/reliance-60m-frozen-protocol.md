## Fixed rules

Assume source timestamps label the **start** of a one-minute bar. Separately assume first use at 60, 120 and 300 seconds after inferred completion. None of these timings is verified. At each candidate source bar `i`, the decision is `i+1+delay/60` minute boundaries after its source timestamp. All features end at `i`: baseline A requires `close[i] > close[i-15]`; candidate B also requires sum of volume `i-4..i` at least twice the median of the same five-bar clock-bucket sums from the **prior 20 distinct source sessions**. All 20 must contain the complete bucket; the current session is excluded. No threshold search. Select the earliest qualifying A and B observation separately per session. If a required reference or continuous-session outcome is missing, censor and keep searching within that session; report the censored count. A and B may have different decision times; paired daily net comparisons include zero for no selection.

The entry price reference is the first full minute-bar close after assumed decision; the 60-minute exit reference is the close at decision plus 60 minutes. An outcome is eligible only if the full window ends within the observed continuous session, excluding auction and session crossing. Research favorable high and adverse low ranges are measured only after the entry reference and are not executable prices. The same selected decisions receive secondary 30-minute and remaining-continuous-session close descriptions where mature. No candle bar becomes a quote, tick or fill.

Chronology: use May–July sessions before July 31 as the initial fit population; July 31 is a whole-session embargo; score the 42 August–September sessions sequentially. At each evaluation date, fit only on earlier sessions whose full 60-minute labels matured before a one-calendar-day embargo. Reuse `research.walkforward.make_folds` to enforce session grouping, label-horizon purging and the embargo. Probability estimates use Laplace smoothing of eligible prior selected outcomes; no fitting on the scored session. Report Brier score, log loss, predicted/observed event rates and movement distributions for favorable and adverse events. October has already been inspected and is omitted. The frozen November 15-minute experiment is untouched.
